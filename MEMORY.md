# Project Memory: Yuga

## Story & Lore Reference
- **Protagonist**: Yuga (Prince of the parallel world Aryavarta-Loka, sent across dimensions by his father Maharaja Devavrata, raised by Hindu & Buddhist sages in the Himalayas).
- **Core Relic**: **Divya-Yuga Mani** (Sacred solar pendant forged in the parallel world, infused with Surya & Trideva blessings; its arrival 1,000 years early sparked human technological and ecological evolution).
- **Earthly Setting**: 31st century. As humanity explored and colonized the stars, the only peoples remaining on Earth are those of Indian Dharmic lineage (**Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs**) who chose to stay as custodians of **Bhumi Devi** (Sacred Earth).
- **Parallel World**: **Aryavarta-Loka** (A dimensional realm worshipping the Trideva—Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva—and practicing Vedic celestial science).
- **Antagonist**: **Kali Asura** and the **Andhaka Void** (cosmic entropy and darkness spreading across dimensions).
- **Sacred Indian Geography**: 
  - Mount Kailash & Lake Mansarovar (Nexus and resting place of the Mani)
  - Varanasi / Kashi (Eternal city, Jyotirlinga subterranean sanctum)
  - Kedarnath & Badrinath (Austerity, Tapasya, martial trials)
  - Bodh Gaya & Nalanda (Trial of Maya, mindfulness, piercing illusions)
- **Mythological Powers & Astras**:
  - Kundalini 7-Chakra Awakening
  - Surya Kavacha (Golden celestial armor)
  - Divyastras (Agneyastra, Varunastra, Vayuvyastra, Sudarshana resonance, Rudra-Tejas)
- Kalaripayattu & Mallayuddha martial mastery
  
## Visual Style & Art Direction Reference
- **Document**: `STYLE.md` (`vedic-pictorial-map-v1`)
- **Art Style**: **Vedic Illuminated Cartography** / Sacred Pictorial Map (*Tirtha Yatra Pata*).
- **Aesthetic Fusion**: Indian Miniature & Pilgrimage Painting (Pahari, Patachitra, Pichwai, Mandalas) + Golden Age Pictorial Cartography (illustrated 3/4 axonometric landmark vignettes) + 31st-Century Vedic Solar-punk (golden prana conduits vs Andhaka Void corruption).
- **Core Engine Stack**: libGDX 2D Engine with 10-layer depth stacking order, 1920x1080 virtual viewport, and custom shaders (parchment, river flow, prana bloom, void miasma).
